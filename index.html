How this was built

What got cleaned, and what to distrust

A calendar is a log of intentions, not a passport. These are the judgment calls made turning it into numbers, so you can overrule any of them.

Rules applied to every event

Home is the default. Chicago and Wheaton events are Home, and so is every day with nothing on the calendar. That means all 1,431 days are accounted for and "away" is a real complement rather than "logged".

Nights, not days. A stay counts as end date minus start date, so a Friday to Sunday trip is 2 nights.

Overlaps get trimmed, not merged. Where one stay ran past the start of the next, the earlier one was cut back to the day the next began. That happens 48 times, mostly on the fast runs through Thailand, Bali and Peru.

Room changes are not new trips. The four Koh Tao events of Dec 2022 (cottage, upgrade, downgrade, hut) are one continuous fortnight on one island. Same for the hotel booking inside the Hanoi stay and "Thanksgiving in AZ" running into "Arizona".

Side trips are nested, not double counted. Nine stays sit entirely inside a longer one, like Ha Long Bay inside Hanoi, Lake Atitlán inside Antigua, Morocco inside Tarifa. Each is listed separately and its nights are subtracted from the surrounding stay.

Calendar dates win. The Salt Lake stay runs Jan 31 to Mar 10 2024 as entered, 39 nights. The Airbnb blurb pasted into that event says a shorter window; it's ignored.

Arizona is travel, not home. The 67-night Nov 2023 to Jan 2024 stretch and the 39-night Salt Lake one are winter bases, so they sit at the top of the longest-stays list without being anything like a trip.

Reminders were dropped. Four events were notes to self rather than stays, things like "Parents Arrive!" and "Leave for FLL".

Two locations were inferred. "Sonderberg" read as Sønderborg in Denmark, and "Antigua" as Antigua in Guatemala rather than Antigua and Barbuda, since Lake Atitlán sits inside that stay. Both worth a second look.

Places kept their calendar names. "Phoenix" and "Arizona" stay separate because you logged them separately, as do "Decatur, IL" and "Decatur / Hillsboro, IL". Say the word and they merge.

Upcoming trips excluded. Lisbon in September, Sri Lanka in October and Casa Shambala in November are on the calendar but haven't happened.

The one number to distrust

Home nights are inferred, not recorded. Any day you travelled without logging it counts here as a night in Wheaton, so 898 nights away is a floor, not a ceiling. The effect is small in 2022 and 2023, where the calendar is nearly continuous, and largest in 2024 and 2025, where the longest unlogged stretch runs 53 days.
